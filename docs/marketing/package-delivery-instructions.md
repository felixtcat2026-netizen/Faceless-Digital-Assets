# Package Delivery Instructions

Purpose: include these instructions with every paid product package so buyers know what to do after purchase, how the deliverables fit together, and what to use first.

Use this file as the master buyer-facing onboarding guide. Copy the relevant product section into the delivered ZIP, Notion page, PDF, email, or customer portal for each package.

## After Purchase

1. Check your purchase email for the download or access link.
2. Save the package folder somewhere easy to find.
3. Open the `Start Here` instructions before editing any templates.
4. Pick the product section below that matches your purchase.
5. Complete the setup steps in order instead of jumping straight into customization.
6. Use the launch checklist to confirm that your offer, copy, delivery path, and follow-up are connected before publishing.

If a package includes multiple formats, use only the format that matches your workflow. Do not rebuild the same asset in every format unless you need channel-specific versions.

## How The Packages Fit Together

The storefront products are designed to work as a simple launch system:

1. Creator Launch Kit: turns a loose idea into a checkout-ready offer.
2. Carousel Copy Bundle: turns that offer into social content that drives traffic.
3. Notion Ops Dashboard: keeps launches, content, fulfillment, and follow-up organized.
4. Starter Pack: gives new leads a low-friction first step before they buy.

Recommended flow:

1. Use the Creator Launch Kit to clarify the offer, audience, price, and delivery promise.
2. Use the Notion Ops Dashboard to create the launch workspace and track production tasks.
3. Use the Carousel Copy Bundle to create 3 to 5 social posts that point to the product page or starter pack.
4. Send cold traffic to the starter pack and warmer traffic to the matching product page.
5. After purchase, deliver the product link, setup instructions, and a short next-step email.
6. Track questions, objections, and results in the dashboard so the next launch improves.

## Creator Launch Kit Instructions

Use this first if you do not yet have a clear sellable offer.

### What You Should Have

- Offer positioning worksheet
- Pricing prompts
- Launch checklist
- Landing page copy prompts
- Fulfillment handoff templates
- 7-day quick-start sprint outline

### Setup Steps

1. Open the offer positioning worksheet.
2. Fill in the audience, pain point, outcome, and promise.
3. Choose one product format and one delivery method.
4. Use the pricing prompts to choose a simple first price.
5. Draft the product page using the landing page copy prompts.
6. Confirm the buyer receives a clear next step immediately after purchase.
7. Run the launch checklist before publishing.

### Tie-It-Together Checklist

- Offer promise matches the product page headline.
- Product price matches the checkout configuration.
- Product deliverables match what the buyer receives after purchase.
- Checkout success email includes the access link and this instruction guide.
- Social posts point to either the product page or starter pack.
- Follow-up email asks whether the buyer used the asset and what blocked them.

## Carousel Copy Bundle Instructions

Use this when you have an offer and need posts that move people toward clicks, replies, opt-ins, or purchases.

### What You Should Have

- Hook formulas
- Body slide frameworks
- CTA endings
- Swipeable examples
- Promotion, authority, and lead-generation angles
- Extra CTA prompts for clicks, replies, saves, and lead magnet signups

### Setup Steps

1. Pick one offer or lead magnet to promote.
2. Choose the funnel stage: awareness, consideration, or conversion.
3. Select a hook formula that matches the audience pain.
4. Build the post using the matching slide or thread framework.
5. Add one CTA only: comment, click, reply, save, or buy.
6. Publish the strongest version first.
7. Save responses and objections for the next post.

### Tie-It-Together Checklist

- The post topic matches the product promise.
- The CTA points to the correct destination.
- The final slide or closing line tells the reader exactly what to do next.
- The caption repeats the core CTA.
- The product page repeats the same promise used in the post.
- If the post is cold traffic, send readers to the starter pack first.

## Notion Ops Dashboard Instructions

Use this when you need one place to manage launches, content, fulfillment, and follow-up.

### What You Should Have

- Launch planning workspace
- Production pipeline views
- Weekly cadence view
- Sales follow-up view
- Fulfillment visibility view
- SOP prompts
- Weekly review template

### Setup Steps

1. Duplicate the dashboard into your Notion workspace.
2. Create one launch record for your current offer.
3. Add all product deliverables as production tasks.
4. Add social posts, emails, and sales page tasks to the launch plan.
5. Add fulfillment tasks for what happens after checkout.
6. Schedule one weekly review.
7. Update status fields at the end of each work session.

### Tie-It-Together Checklist

- Every product has a launch record.
- Every deliverable has an owner, status, and due date.
- Every purchase has a fulfillment step.
- Every checkout has a post-purchase follow-up step.
- Every campaign has social posts, email copy, and a product page link.
- Weekly review notes capture what sold, what stalled, and what to improve.

## Starter Pack Instructions

Use this as the first step for new leads who are not ready to buy.

### What You Should Have

- Starter templates
- Launch checklist
- 7-day plan
- First-offer worksheet or prompt set

### Setup Steps

1. Open the 7-day plan.
2. Pick one lightweight product idea.
3. Use the starter templates to draft the first version.
4. Use the checklist to find missing offer details.
5. Choose the paid product that best matches your blocker.
6. Save your notes so they can be reused in the paid package.

### Tie-It-Together Checklist

- The starter pack points to the best paid next step.
- Email follow-up references the buyer's selected blocker.
- Product recommendations match the buyer's stage.
- The free resource teaches one useful action without replacing the paid package.

## Post-Purchase Email Template

Subject: Your access is ready

Hi {{first_name}},

Your {{offer_name}} access is ready here:

{{access_link}}

Start with the instructions first:

{{instructions_link}}

The fastest way to use this is:

1. Open the Start Here guide.
2. Complete the setup steps for your package.
3. Use the checklist before publishing or sharing the offer.

If you are not sure what to do first, reply with the blocker that best matches your situation:

- I need a clearer offer.
- I need better content that drives traffic.
- I need a system to manage the launch.
- I need help choosing the next step.

You do not need to use every file today. Start with the first setup step, then move through the checklist.

## Package Folder Structure

Each delivered product should include this minimum structure:

```text
01_Start_Here/
  README.md
  package-delivery-instructions.md
02_Templates/
03_Examples/
04_Checklists/
05_Bonus/
```

For Notion products, include a public duplicate link and a PDF or Markdown copy of the setup steps.

For copy bundles, include editable text files plus example exports.

For visual products, include source files, export files, and recommended dimensions.

## Fulfillment QA Before Publishing

Before a package is sold, confirm:

- The checkout product name matches the delivered package name.
- The buyer receives access immediately after purchase.
- The access link works in a private browser.
- The package includes this instruction guide or a product-specific version of it.
- The first file or page clearly says what to do first.
- The package does not contain placeholder links, broken images, or unfinished examples.
- The post-purchase email includes both the access link and instructions link.
