# Funnel Content Assets By Channel And Stage

## Objective
Build a publish-ready content pack for TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU that drives discovery, trust, and conversion for faceless digital products.

## Stage/Channel Matrix
| Funnel stage | Goal | Primary channels | KPI target |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | Reach new audiences and earn first click | Short-form video, carousel, SEO blog | Views, saves, profile visits, CTR |
| MOFU | Build trust and move to consideration | Short-form video, carousel, SEO blog | Watch time, comments, lead magnet opt-ins |
| BOFU | Convert warm prospects into buyers | Short-form video, carousel, SEO blog | Checkout starts, conversion rate, revenue |

## Asset Briefs
### Short-form video brief
- Visual direction: fast cuts, bold captions, proof overlays (dashboard snippets, template previews), 9:16 framing.
- Structure: 0-2s hook, 3-20s value/proof, 21-35s CTA.
- CTA style: one action only ("save", "comment", or "buy") based on stage.
- Output package: script, shot list, caption, on-screen text, thumbnail frame.

### Carousel brief
- Visual direction: high-contrast cover slide, one idea per slide, progress cues ("1/7", "2/7"), clean CTA final slide.
- Structure: cover -> insight/problem -> examples/proof -> action step -> CTA.
- CTA style: TOFU save/share, MOFU comment/DM, BOFU click/buy.
- Output package: slide copy, layout notes, design references, caption, hashtags.

### Blog/SEO brief
- Visual direction: scannable sections, comparison tables, checklists, in-line CTA modules.
- Structure: search-intent intro -> solution framework -> examples/proof -> FAQ -> CTA.
- CTA style: TOFU lead magnet, MOFU validation toolkit, BOFU product purchase.
- Output package: target keyword, title, meta description, H2/H3 outline, internal links, CTA blocks.

## Publishing Checklist
- Match each asset to one funnel stage and one KPI before publishing.
- Keep CTA singular and stage-appropriate.
- Repackage each blog into one carousel + one short-form video within 48 hours.
- Review weekly winners by watch time, saves, and conversion to update next batch.
